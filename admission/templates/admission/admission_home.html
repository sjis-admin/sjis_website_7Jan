Applicant Documents

  • Birth Certificate

    Attested copy of the official Government Birth Registration Certificate.

  • Recent Passport Photographs

    Four (4) recent studio-quality passport-size color photographs of the applicant.

  • Academic Records & Transfer Certificate (TC)

    Previous year's grade report card and clearance/transfer certificate from the former school.

Parent & Guardian Records

  • National ID or Passport

    Clear photocopies of National ID cards or Passports of both parents/legal guardians.

  • Parent Photographs

    One (1) passport-size color photograph of each parent/guardian for security records.

  • Medical & Immunization Card

    Basic medical clearance form and emergency health contact information.
