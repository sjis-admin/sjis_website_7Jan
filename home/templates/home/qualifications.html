Qualifications We Offer

Secondary Stage

Cambridge Ordinary Level

General Certificate of Education (O-Level)

The Cambridge Ordinary Level (O-Level) is an internationally recognised qualification designed for 14–16 year olds. It develops students' skills in creative thinking, enquiry and problem solving and prepares them thoroughly for the next stage of education.

Core Subject Areas

Mathematics Physics Chemistry Biology English Language English Literature Computer Science Business Studies Economics

Age Group

14 – 16 Years

Duration

2 Years (Grades 9 – 10)

Grading

A* – E Scale

Awarding Body

CAIE (Centre BD084)

Cambridge O-Level is globally recognised as equivalent to the UK GCSE and accepted as a prerequisite for A-Level study, the IB Diploma, and university entry in 160+ countries.

Advanced Stage

Cambridge International A-Level

AS & A2 Level (Advanced Subsidiary & Advanced Level)

Cambridge International A-Levels are recognised by the world's best universities as proof of academic excellence. The programme develops deep subject knowledge and critical thinking skills, providing a robust foundation for higher education worldwide.

Core Subject Areas

Mathematics Physics Chemistry Further Mathematics Biology

Age Group

16 – 19 Years

Duration

2 Years (Grades 11 – 12)

Grading

A* – E Scale

Awarding Body

CAIE (Centre BD084)

Cambridge A-Levels are equivalent to UK A-Levels and are accepted for university admissions in the UK, USA, Canada, Australia, and across the world. Completion of O-Level (or equivalent) is required for entry.
